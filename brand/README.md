# Card Creator 图标

`icon.png` 是由内置 imagegen 生成并等比缩小的 512 × 512 主图标，`../favicon.png` 是同一图标的 64 × 64 网站标签版本。两者是本项目自创视觉标识，不包含第三方支付或交通品牌。

生成提示词：

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: square visual identity icon for an open-source 'Card Creator' image-generation skill and its gallery website; must remain legible as a browser favicon at 32 pixels. Produce ONE clean, high-end icon, not a presentation sheet or mockup. Composition: a bold centered rounded rectangular card silhouette, slightly tilted, in deep midnight navy on a solid warm-ivory square background. Across the card, ONE confident flowing painterly ribbon/stroke transitions from vivid cobalt blue to coral orange and butter yellow, suggesting a blank card becoming artwork. Flat vector-friendly shapes, minimal details, strong optical balance and generous outer padding. The card shape should occupy roughly 70% of the square. Editorial art-gallery character rather than fintech startup or banking logo. No text, no letters, no numbers, no credit-card chip, no NFC/contactless waves, no currency signs, no copyrighted marks, no outlines thinner than can survive at 32 pixels, no shadow, no device or web-page mockup. Full solid opaque square canvas without transparency. Provide only the icon artwork.
